The **Legal Links** section is ideal for placing links to your Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, or Cookie Policy. This widget is designed to be managed visually and easily.

---

## 1. Widget Activation

By default, this section is disabled in the control panel. To enable it:

* Go to the **Layout** section in your Blogger dashboard.
* Look for the gadget named **"Enlaces Legales"** (or its equivalent in your dashboard language).
* Ensure the **"Show this widget"** switch is turned on (green).

---

## 2. Adding New Links

To add your legal pages, you don't need to write HTML code; just follow these steps:

1. Click on the **"ADD A NEW ITEM"** option.
2. In **"Site name"**, type the page title (example: Privacy Policy).
3. In **"Site URL"**, paste the corresponding link to your legal page.
4. Click **Save** on both the item and the main widget.

---

## 3. Organization and Order

If you have multiple links (for example: Privacy Policy, Terms, and Cookies), you can use the arrows next to each item to move them up or down in the list. You can also choose an automatic sorting option:

* **Don't sort**: Maintains the manual order you choose.
* **Alphabetical order**: Organizes the links from A to Z automatically.

---

> **Note:** These links usually appear at the very bottom of your template (the Footer), maintaining a discreet and elegant design that does not interfere with your blog's main content.
