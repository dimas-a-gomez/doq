The **Social Media** widget allows you to add interactive icons to the footer that direct your users to your favorite platforms.

---

## 1. Widget Activation

This section uses an **HTML/JavaScript** component:

* Enter the **Layout** panel in Blogger.
* Locate the widget titled **"Redes Sociales"** (or its equivalent in your dashboard) in the footer area.
* Toggle the **"Show this widget"** switch to ON.

---

## 2. Obtaining Icons (Meteor Icons)

To make the icons look professional and consistent, you must get them from the official library:

1. Visit the **[Meteor Icons](https://meteoricons.com/)** website.
2. Search for the social network you need (e.g., Instagram, Facebook, YouTube).
3. **Copy the SVG code**:
   * **On PC**: Hover over the icon and click the copy button.
   * **On Mobile**: Tap directly on the icon to copy the code to your clipboard.

---

## 3. Content Configuration

Paste the base structure into the **"Content"** field of the widget and replace the `<svg>...</svg>` block with the one you copied previously:

```html
<a href='#url' aria-label='#social-name' class='social-icon'>
    <svg>...</svg>
</a>
