By default, the **MountainB** menu is disabled so you can build it from scratch according to your project's needs.

---

## 1. Menu Activation

To start adding links, we must first enable the component in the control panel:

* Go to the **Layout** section in your Blogger dashboard.
* Locate the gadget named **"Menú"** (or its equivalent in your dashboard language).
* Ensure the display toggle is set to **ON**.

---

## 2. Adding Links and Pages

Once active, you can start structuring your navigation:

1. Click the **"ADD A NEW ITEM"** button.
2. In the **"Page name"** field, type the text your users will see (example: Home).
3. In the **"Page URL"** field, paste the corresponding link.
4. Click **Save** to confirm the item.

---

## 3. Customization with Icons (Meteor Icons)

To give your menu a modern style, you can add icons before the text.

### Steps to insert icons:
* Visit the official [Meteor Icons](https://meteoricons.com/) library.
* Search for the icon that best fits your section.
* **Copy the code:**
    * **On PC:** Hover over the icon and click the copy SVG button.
    * **On Mobile:** Tap directly on the icon to copy it.
* **Insert in Blogger:** In the **"Page name"** field, paste the SVG code you copied first, followed by the menu name.

> **Example:** `<svg>...</svg> Home`

---

Done! Once you save the changes in the gadget and the overall Blogger layout, you will see your custom menu working with professional icons.
