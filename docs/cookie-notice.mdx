The **Cookie Notice** is an essential component for informing your users about data usage, presented with an elegant design that appears at the bottom of the screen.

---

## 1. Widget Activation

Like other advanced components, this widget is disabled by default:

* Go to the **Layout** section in your Blogger dashboard.
* Look for the **HTML/JavaScript** gadget named **"Aviso de Cookies"** (or its translated equivalent in your layout), located at the end of the list.
* Toggle the **"Show this widget"** switch.

---

## 2. Content Configuration

For the notice to function and display correctly, you must use the template's specific HTML structure.

### Base Code:

```html
<div id="cookie-notice-container">
    <h3 class="cookie-title">Cookie Notice</h3>
    <p class="cookie-desc">Cookies are crunchier and more delicious when you enjoy them.</p>
    <div class="cookie-buttons">
        <button id="accept-cookies" class="cookie-btn btn-accept">Delicious</button>
        <a href="#url" class="cookie-btn btn-info">Learn More</a>
    </div>
</div>
