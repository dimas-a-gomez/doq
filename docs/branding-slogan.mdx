The **Branding & Slogan** section allows you to display your project name, a brief description, and a direct contact link at the bottom of the site.

---

## 1. Widget Activation

By default, this component is disabled. To enable it:

* Access the **Layout** panel in Blogger.
* Look for the HTML/JavaScript widget titled **"Marca y Slogan"** (or its equivalent in your dashboard).
* Toggle the switch to **ON**.

---

## 2. Content Configuration

You must paste the following code into the **"Content"** field of the widget and replace the values marked with `#`:

### Base Code:

```html
<h2 class='brand-name'>#name</h2>
<p class='brand-slogan'>#slogan</p>
<a class='brand-email' href='mailto:your@email.com'>#email@</a>
